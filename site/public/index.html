YouTube Music, drawn natively.

Tonearm is a client for YouTube Music on Windows and Android, written in Rust. No web view and no browser engine, so it starts fast and stays small.

Download for Windows Download for Android

What it does

Everything you'd expect from a music app, drawn by the app itself.

Synced lyrics

Timed by the syllable where the source allows, with duet sides and out of focus lines.

Crossfade and mixes

Crossfade, volume levelling and beat-matched transitions you can edit per playlist.

Cast to your TV and speakers

Chromecasts, Google TVs, Nest speakers and groups, with Tonearm's own screen showing the song and lyrics.

One account, every device

Sign in with an emailed code or a QR code. Your settings and pins follow you from computer to phone.

Offline and resume

Keep songs on your device, and pick up where you left off: the queue, the song and the position.

In the car and on your wrist

Android Auto with Home, Explore, Library and Downloads, and controls on a Galaxy Watch.

Moving covers

Animated album covers and Spotify Canvas clips, on pages and in Now playing.

Updates itself

Both apps check the releases page and install the new version for you.

Download

Free and open source. Both apps update themselves after the first install.

Windows

Installs just for you and needs no admin rights. The installer isn't code signed, so SmartScreen may warn the first time: pick More info, then Run anyway.

Get the installer

Android

Android 8 or later. Your browser or file manager will ask to allow installing from it, the first time only.

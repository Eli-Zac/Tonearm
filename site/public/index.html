Tonearm
Features Download GitHub

YouTube Music, drawn natively.

Tonearm is a client for YouTube Music on Windows and Android, written in Rust. No web view and no browser engine, so it starts fast and stays small.

Download for Windows Download for Android

What it does

Everything you'd expect from a music app, drawn by the app itself. Here is a closer look, with screenshots from the Windows app.



Synced lyrics, big and in time

Now playing shows the cover beside lyrics that follow the song. The current line is bright, the lines around it fade and blur out, and the whole page takes its colours from the cover.

  • Timed by the syllable where the source allows
  • Duet sides, and a dots line for instrumental breaks
  • Translation, if you turn it on in Customization
  • Animated covers and Spotify Canvas clips


Home, with Now playing beside it

Home, Explore and your library sit in the side menu. Open the Now playing panel and the cover and the next lyrics stay at the side while you browse.

  • Mood chips, new releases, mixes and charts
  • Jump back in and Recently played, picked up from what you play
  • Your library, liked songs and playlists once you sign in
  • Pins in the side menu for the albums and artists you keep going back to


Search as you type

Suggestions show up as you type, with queries, artists, albums and songs together, so you can get to a song without finishing the word.

  • Move through the list with the arrow keys, Enter to search
  • Songs, videos, albums and artists in the suggestions


Artist, album and playlist pages

A big banner, a play button in the accent colour, and the popular songs numbered underneath. Albums and playlists get the same treatment.

  • Play, shuffle and the more menu, on every page
  • Play next and Add to queue from any song's menu
  • A queue you can drag to reorder, with radio, smart shuffle and autoplay


Crossfade, levelling and mixes

As a song ends, the next one fades in while it fades out. Volume levelling plays every song at about the same loudness, using YouTube's own measure of each one.

  • Beat-matched transitions you can edit per playlist
  • A sleep timer next to the volume
  • Skipping a song still switches at once


One account, every device

A Tonearm account keeps your settings and pins together, so the phone looks like the computer. Sign in with an emailed code, or scan a QR code from the other device.

  • YouTube Music for your library and recommendations
  • Spotify, for its Canvas clips
  • Last.fm scrobbling and Discord Rich Presence

And more



Cast to your TV and speakers

Chromecasts, Google TVs, Nest speakers and groups. Mixes and crossfades carry over, and a TV shows Tonearm's own screen with the song and lyrics.



Offline and resume

Keep songs on your device, and pick up where you left off: the queue, the song and the position. Songs you play again start at once.



In the car and on your wrist

The phone app has Android Auto with Home, Explore, Library and Downloads, and controls on a Galaxy Watch.



Made for Windows

Media keys, the media flyout, play controls under the taskbar preview, and closing to the tray while it keeps playing.



Updates itself

Both apps check the releases page and install the new version for you, after checking it against GitHub's own SHA-256.



Settings that stay out of the way

Accounts, Playback, Customization, and Storage and history, each a short page of plain options.

Download

Free and open source. Both apps update themselves after the first install.



Windows

Installs just for you and needs no admin rights. The installer isn't code signed, so SmartScreen may warn the first time: pick More info, then Run anyway.
